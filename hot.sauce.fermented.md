Fermented Hot Sauce Recipe
--------------------------
>Basic fermented hot sauce recipe.

Ingredients
-----------
1. 454 grams / 16 ounces of peppers.
2. 1/2 large onion.
3. 6 cloves of garlic.
4. 6 sprigs of cilantro ( optional ).
5. 1/2 cup vinegar.
6. 2 limes.

Preparation
-----------
1. Slice peppers, removing stems.
2. Slice onion.
3. Slice garlic.

Fermentation
------------
1. Place peppers, onion and garlic into container.
2. Add 4 tablespoons of kosher or sea salt.
3. Add 4 cups of water.
4. Ferment for 1 to 3 weeks.

Blend
-----
1. Remove the fermented peppers, onion and garlic from container, saving the brine.
2. Place fermented peppers, onion and garlic in blender.
3. Add vinegar.
4. Slice lime; and squeeze into blender.
5. Optionally add brine to obtain desired consistency.
