Fermented Hot Sauce Recipe
--------------------------
>Basic fermented hot sauce recipe.

Ingredients
-----------
1. 454 grams / 16 ounces of peppers.
2. 1/2 large onion.
3. 6 cloves of garlic.

Preparation
-----------
1. Slice peppers, removing stems.
2. Slice onion.
3. Peel garlic cloves.

Fermentation
------------
1. Place ingredients in fermentation container.
2. Add 4 cups of water.
3. Add 4 teaspons of kosher salt.
4. Ferment for 1 to 2+ weeks.

Blend
-----
1. Remove the fermented peppers, onion and garlic from fermentation container, saving the brine.
2. Place fermented peppers, onion and garlic in blender.
3. Blend to desired consistency, optionally adding brine as required.

Bottle
------
1. Optionally strain sauce, separating pulp from sauce.
2. Pour sauce into ***6*** 5 ounce jars or alternate container(s).
3. Store in frig or pantry.
