Hot Sauce Recipe
----------------
>Basic hot sauce recipe.

Ingredients
-----------
1. 227 grams / 8 ounces of peppers.
2. 1/2 cup vinegar.
3. 3 grams / 1 teaspoon sea salt.

Preparation
-----------
1. Remove pepper stems.

Cook
----
1. Roast peppers in oven @475 for 15+ minutes.

Blend
-----
1. Add roasted peppers to blender.
2. Add vinegar to blender.
3. Blend to desired consistency, adding vinegar as required.

Bottle
------
1. Pour hot sauce into ***3+*** 5 ounce woozy jars or alternate container(s).
2. Store in fridge or pantry.
