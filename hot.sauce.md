Hot Sauce Recipe
----------------
>Basic hot sauce recipe.

Ingredients
-----------
1. 227 grams / 8 ounces of peppers.
2. 1/2 cup vinegar.
3. 3 grams / 1 teaspoon sea salt.

Preparation
-----------
1. Slice peppers, removing stems.

Cook
----
1. Add peppers, vinegar and salt to pot.
2. Bring to boil; then simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Allow sauce to cool.
>Optionally ***roast*** peppers, onion and garlic.

Blend
-----
1. Place ingredients in blender.
2. Squeeze sliced lime into blender.
3. Pour vinegar into blender.
4. Blend to desired consistency.

Bottle
------
1. Optionally strain sauce, separating pulp from sauce.
2. Pour sauce into ***3*** 5 ounce jars or alternate container(s).
3. Store in frig or pantry.
