Kimchi Recipe
-------------
>Basic kimchi recipe. Study Korean chefs for ideas on how they make kimchi.

Ingredients
-----------
1. Napa Cabbage ( 1 head )
2. Momoya Kimuchi no Moto **Kimchee Base** ( https://tinyurl.com/4a3wuucz )
3. Gochujang ( Korean Hot Pepper Paste ) ( https://tinyurl.com/2s3bnr2e )
4. Fish Sauce ( https://tinyurl.com/yrf73brc )

Preparation
-----------
1. Silce cabbage into bite-size pieces.

Brine
-----
1. Place sliced cabbage in a large bowl.
2. Sprinkle kosher salt over cabbage.
3. Fill bowl with cool water.
4. Let the cabbage soak for 2+ hours.

Sauce
-----
1. Pour 6+ oz, or 170+ grams, of kimchi base in a bowl.
2. Add 2 tablespoons, or 30 grams, of gochujang.
3. Add 2 tablespoons, or 30 grams, of fish sauce.
4. Mix thoroughly.

Build
-----
1. Drain and rinse off cabbage, keeping cabbage in bowl.
2. Place a portion of the drained cabbage in a bowl.
3. Add 3+ tablespoons of kimchi sauce to cabbage.
4. Mix kimchi sauce with cabbage. ( wearing plastic gloves )
5. Place freshly sauced cabbage into a kimchi container.
6. Repeat steps 2 to 4 as required.
>Ideally, invest in a Korean kimchi container.

Fermentation
------------
1. Place kimchi container inside fridge.
2. Allow kimchi to ferment for 3+ days before eating. ( ideally ferment for at least 7 days )
3. Eat kimchi.
>For faster initial fermentation, place kimchi container in shaded kitchen area for 1-2 days.
