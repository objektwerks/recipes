Skillet Pizza Recipe
--------------------
>Basic 12" cast iron skillet, with cast iron lid, pizza recipe.

Ingredients
-----------
1. 144 grams water @ 100F / 37C degrees, @ 60% hydration.
2. 3 grams yeast ( 1 teaspoon ).
3. 240 grams bread flour.
4. 3 grams sea salt ( 1 teaspoon ).

Dough
-----
1. Pour water @ 100F / 37C degrees into a bowl.
2. Mix in yeast.
3. Mix in flour.
4. Mix in sea salt.
5. Mix in spritz of olive oil.
6. Knead dough into ball.
7. Remove dough ball from bowl and place on board - knead into smooth ball, using flour as required.

Fermentation
------------
1. Add olive oil to bottom of coverable container.
2. Place dough ball in coverable container - then cover.
2. Ferment dough ball for 2+ hours @ room temperature.
>Optionally, ferment dough ball overnight in fridge.

Shape
-----
1. Spread olive oil in skillet.
2. Place dough in skillet.
3. Stretch dough evenly thoughout skillet.

Oven
----
1. Pre-heat oven @ 450 degrees.

Toppings
--------
1. Tomato sauce.
2. Shredded mozarella.
>Alter toppings as desired.

Build
-----
1. Add toppings and seasonings.
>Optionally, add shredded mozarella first for this style.

Bake
----
1. Cover skillet with lid and place in oven.
2. Bake @ 10 minutes.
3. Inspect pizza crust and top intermittingly.
4. Remove skillet lid from skillet and bake @ 3 minutes.
5. Remove skillet from oven.

Crust
-----
>If the crust is still not brown and crispy:
1. Heat stove burner @ medium-low heat.
2. Place skillet on burner until crust is brown and crispy.
